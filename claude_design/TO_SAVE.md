### Lessons (2026-09-30)
- Running 3 generations in parallel burned the whole usage limit. Do ONE at a time.
- The good models came from a detailed 2nd-pass redesign prompt. The first prompt must be concrete about shapes: silhouette, head shape, plates/scales, "tapered organic shapes, never plain boxes", and "one pass, skip verification passes" (saves usage).
- Start each creature in a NEW project (continuing a long chat re-sends ~100k context).

### THE RECIPE THAT MAKES THEM GOOD (found 2026-09-30, from the Forest Fawn / Vine Serpent chats)
The 4 perfect ones (Forest Stag, Vine Serpent, Treant, Mossback v2) were made in 2 or 3 rounds in ONE project:
1. A simple base prompt: "a <animal> pet ... Stylized low-poly Roblox style with bright colours" + parts + anims + VFX. Let it do its own render checks (do NOT say "skip verification").
2. The same project: "This is too cute for my game. Please redesign it as a fierce, cool <Name>: <muscular/dark/glowing eyes/details>. Keep the named parts and animations..."
One-shot fierce prompts (Kappa, Spark Fox, Raiju v1, Storm Ram, Thunder Bull, Thunderbird, Storm Wyvern v1) came out blocky and not semi-realistic, so REDO them with the recipe.
