## S1 - SITE: the Ohmu run (*Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind*), multi-leg gait
`/tags/creature-animation` -> Sakugabooru **#270689** (tags `takashi_nakamura`, `nausicaa_of_the_valley_of_the_wind`, `creatures`,
`running`; source "BD (Ohmu AD: Tadakatsu Yoshida)"). The shot used is the Ohmu legs close-up (front view, on sand), 82.9-85.2 s
= frames f1987-f2042 at 23.976 fps. The drawings change on twos.

| # | observation | frames |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | **Two alternating leg sets.** At every drawing roughly half of each side's legs are planted wide and the other half are arched high in swing. From one drawing to the next (f1991 -> f1993 -> f1995 ...) the planted and swinging legs swap, set by set. Neighbouring legs on one side are never in the same phase. | f1991-f2001 |
| O2 | **The shell stays level.** Its top edge holds the same height while the legs cycle. The motion is all in the legs (the same as C1 R1). | f1987-f2042 |
| O3 | The swing legs arch high because this is a run (a set swaps about every 4 frames, a cycle of about 8 = ~10 f at 30 fps). At a walk the same alternation runs slower and lower. | f1991-f1999 |

**Note (review 4):** the Ohmu shot is a RUN, so it backs the leg alternation and the level shell, not the walk timing.

**Walk check (the review asked):** the site gait agrees with the Wikimedia read: alternating leg sets (= our tripods
L1/R5/L3 vs R4/L2/R6), a level shell (bob 0.010), and the legs doing the work. No contradiction, so **the walk is unchanged**.
The Ohmu's high leg arch is a run feature; our 30 f crab walk keeps a low lift, now 0.10 (C1 R1, R4; 0.07 until the 2026-10-28 per-clip audit).
**Updated 2026-10-28 (per-clip audit + round 6):**
- The phone-size read needed more motion than the reference's near-level shell (R1 / O2). The walk now uses:
  - lift **0.10**, carapace bob **0.016** and shell roll **2.4 deg**;
  - a fore-aft pitch of **+/-1.5 deg**, nose up on each tripod contact;
  - crusher sway **6 deg**.
- The gait, the 45 deg body and the C1 timing are unchanged.
- The shell is no longer strictly level: R1 / O2 is a direction, softened for readability at 390 px.

## LEAD RULING (final 8.5 review, CM3): what sets the walk timing
**The walk timing (the 30 f cycle, duty 0.55, the small quick steps) comes from the Wikimedia crab footage C1** (rock
crabs, 4.6-7.0 s: R1 low level shell, R2 diagonal body, R4 small quick alternating groups). The site clip S1 (the
Ohmu RUN) is NOT the timing source. It backs only the alternating leg groups and the level shell (O2), as noted in
review 4. animationreference.org has no crab, insect or multi-leg walk to take a crab timing from.

## C1 - rock crab walk (4.6-7.0 s) (Wikimedia study notes)
| # | observation | time |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | The carapace stays **low and level**, almost no bob; the motion is all in the legs. | all |
| R2 | The crab walks **diagonally**: its body is turned ~30-45 deg to the travel line (supports the 45 deg walk yaw). | 4.6-7.0 |
| R3 | The claws are **held low and folded** in front of the mouth while walking (not raised). | all |
| R4 | Legs step in small, quick alternating groups; the tips stay planted while the body slides over them. | 5.0-6.4 |

## C2 - fiddler crab wave (Wikimedia study notes)

## How each clip uses them
- **Base pose (review 2 M2, lead ruling):** idle, roar and act are keyed with the **Spine at the walk's 45 deg yaw** and
  the feet planted on the yawed rest spots, so the idle <-> walk blend turns nothing (R2).
- **walk (30 f loop):** the tripod gait (L1/R5/L3 vs R4/L2/R6) along the travel line, body at 45 deg (R2), carapace
  bob **0.016** with a 2.4 deg roll and a +/-1.5 deg fore-aft pitch (nose up on the contacts), leg lift **0.10** (2026-10-28:
  R1's level shell softened for the phone-size read, see the walk-check note), claws held 10 deg LOWER than rest and folded
  (R3, review 2 N5), eyes lag 2 f (plan); the crusher sways 6 deg twice per cycle between the tripod contacts
  (final 8.5). Timing: C1 (lead ruling above).
- **idle (120 f loop): claw guard (#7).** The crusher lifts 20 deg to guard and breathes there (9+ round: +/-6 deg over
  one 35 f arc, troughs f45 / f80, peak f62; the body bobs 0.008); the eyestalks swivel +/-10 deg one
  at a time with freezes between (#7); the cutter taps the shell at f70 (`tap`); planted.
- **roar (60 f): clack threat (#6 + #5).** f0-14 the body rises 0.05 H and the claws lift forward 30 deg (review 3 N3) (#6 explode
  from a short crouch f0-4); f14-40 three clacks: each a 2-frame cocked open, a 2-frame snap shut, a hard stop (#5),
  events `clack1` f22, `clack2` f30, `clack3` f38; the mandibles open; f40-60 lower and settle.
- **act_semaphore (120 f, round 5): the fiddler wave (F1-F3 + S2).** (9+ round: the eyestalks track the crusher - L 22 deg, R 30 deg - 2 f behind the wave, the shell rolls -2 +/- 4 deg, the trough drops to merus 2 / palm +2 with each beat, crusher side down at the tops.) f0-18 stand tall: the body rises 0.10 BU with
  pitch -5, and the crusher unfolds up. Three slow display arcs: each top is HELD 8 f (f26-34 / f49-57 / f72-80, H2),
  with the hold centres `wave1` f30, `wave2` f53, `wave3` f76. The down-stroke takes 5 f (H3: the quick drop) and the
  up-sweep 10 f (H1, F3). The top is the claw raised high (F1); the trough at f39 / f62 swings it down and forward in front
  (F2). Top <-> trough rotation in world space: merus 36.7 deg, carpus 42.1 deg, claw 36.7 deg. f80-86 the claw points out
  and holds while the cutter taps twice (`tap1` f90, `tap2` f96; S2's other limb). f96-110 it swings in first, then folds
  down. f110-120 exact rest. Legs planted every frame.
