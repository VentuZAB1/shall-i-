# Brainrot / "Steal a X" Roblox games: progression and economy (2025-2026)

Source-quality note: The official Fandom wikis (stealabrainrot.fandom.com, escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com) returned HTTP 402 to the fetcher, so most numbers come from guide sites (Eldorado, U7BUY, Game Rant, AllThingsHow, games.gg, timesaver.gg). Items marked "(fetched)" were read directly from the page. Items marked "(search summary)" came from search-engine summaries of the listed pages and were not read in full. Treat those with more caution. Many of these guide sites sell Roblox items (Eldorado, U7BUY, games.gg), so they are aggregators, not primary sources.

## Q1. Steal a Brainrot: rarity income, buy costs, rebirth ladder, max rebirth, time to each rebirth

### Takeaway
Income rises roughly 10x per rarity tier: Common $1-14/s up to OG/Secret at $750M-$1B/s. A brainrot's buy price is about 100-1000x its income/s on the low tiers and about 1000x at the top (Strawberry Elephant costs $750B and earns $750M/s). There are 19 rebirths (Rebirth 19 added 15 Aug 2026). Cash costs climb about 3-10x per step, from $500K to $30Qa. Each rebirth also needs one or two specific named brainrots, and that named-unit requirement is the main gate. Each rebirth gives +1x cash multiplier (x0.5 at R1, up to x19), +10s base-lock time, +1 base slot, starting cash and new gear.

**Timing / bottlenecks**
- R13: the $14T cash wall plus Trenostruzzo Turbo 3000, which spawns poorly even among Brainrot Gods, so players "sit on the cash for days waiting on the belt". R16 is the first Secret requirement and that unit rarely appears on the conveyor. R17 is the only level that needs 2 Secrets. At R19 the cash is the wall: "the price tripled while the return shrank" (x18 to x19 for 3x the cost of R18) — (fetched) [U7BUY](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide/)
- Reaching $30Qa for R19 takes "most players several days at x18, even with a full 27-slot base" — (search summary) [U7BUY](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-19/)
- R16 "may take several days or weeks to obtain the required 1Qa" and Los Tralaleritos has a low spawn rate — (search summary) [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-rebirth-16-guide)
- R9-R15 all require Brainrot God units, and this is described as "the longest stretch of the game" — (search summary) [U7BUY](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide/)

### Inferences
- The design uses two gates. Cash grows smoothly, but a named rare unit (which you buy off a shared conveyor or steal from someone else) is a random, social gate. That second gate is what spreads progression over days. Even at R1, cash is cheap ($500K) and the specific units are the real requirement.
- The cost-to-multiplier ratio worsens sharply late. R1 to R10 costs grow about 250,000x while the multiplier grows from x0.5 to x9. The late game is meant to be measured in days or weeks.
- Rough cost-to-income rule: a brainrot pays back its own price in about 100-2,500 seconds at the low tiers and about 1,000s at the top tiers.

### Gaps
- No primary-source (official Fandom) rebirth or rarity table could be fetched (HTTP 402). The exact current rarity ranges for God and Secret are unverified.
- No reliable player-reported times for early rebirths (R1-R8). No Reddit threads were found.
- Exact spawn-rate percentages per rarity, beyond Common's ~55%, were not found.

## Q2. Escape Tsunami for Brainrots: speed costs, zone gating, rebirths, waves, day-1 vs week-1

### Takeaway
Rebirths are gated by the Speed stat, not by cash directly. R1 needs 40 Speed, and each rebirth adds +10 Speed and +0.5x money up to R20 (230 Speed, 11x). Later updates added R21-R26 or more, which need +0.25x each plus base income per second and Divine units in the base. Each rebirth resets Speed, which costs 1.17x more per level. Zones are bands of distance from spawn, with Speed thresholds per rarity.

### Cited Findings
- Rebirth table R1-R20: Speed 40, 50, ... 230. Multiplier 1.5x, 2x, ... 11x (+10 Speed and +0.5x per level). Speed resets to 0 on rebirth. Brainrots, carry upgrades and base are kept (AllThingsHow). The "Skip Rebirth Keep Speed!" pass costs 89 Robux — (fetched) [AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/how-rebirth-works-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-all-20-levels/)
- Sources disagree on whether carry upgrades survive a rebirth. AllThingsHow says they are kept and Beebom says they are lost — (search summary) [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-carry-more-brainrots-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/)
- Game Rant (7 Feb 2026) table: R21 11x/230 Speed. R22 11.25x/240. R23 11.5x/300 + $4.99 Quintillion/s base generation. R24 11.75x/350 + $100Qn/s + 1 Divine in base. R25 (max at that time) 12x/360 + $10 Sextillion/s + 3 Divines. Rebirth becomes mandatory after base level 20 — (fetched) [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/roblox-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-how-rebirth-fast-all-levels/). Its R19 and R20 rows are duplicated (both 10.5x/220), which conflicts with AllThingsHow's R20 = 11x/230.
- Max rebirth has been raised several times. It was 25 (Game Rant, Feb 2026), then 26 at 12.5x (AllThingsHow, 16 Feb 2026), then 27 (Game8), then 29 at 13.25x per the Fandom wiki. From R29 on, the requirements include Jump, money/s and Infinity brainrots in the base — (search summary) [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Roblox/archives/580777), [Fandom Upgrades](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrades)
- Speed max level is 15,670. Each level costs 1.17x the previous one, bought in 1x or 10x steps. Carry max is 7 brainrots (start 1, +1 per level). Base upgrade max is level 40. Brainrot level cap is 250 (200 without R26), with "1.25x per level" income, and the base upgrade price is capped at $25K. Top unit is Meta Technetta (Infinity) at $625M/s base — (fetched) [AllThingsHow max caps](https://allthings.how/every-max-level-cap-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-explained/). The 1.25x-per-level figure compounded over 250 levels is implausible, so it may mean +25% additive per level. Treat it as unverified.
- Carry upgrade cost climbs to about $1B by carry level 3. Beebom says level 3-4 is enough — (search summary) [Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-carry-more-brainrots-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/)
- Zone Speed bands (games.gg, 15 Jun 2026): Common 0-20, Uncommon 20-40, Rare 40-65, Epic 65-85, Legendary 85-110, Mythical 110-130, Cosmic 130-150, Secret 150+, Celestial 150+ — (fetched) [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-zones-guide/)
- Rarity tiers grew over time: an early guide listed 9 tiers and Eldorado now lists 13 (Common to Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical, Cosmic, Secret, Celestial, Divine, Infinity, Paradox, Eternal). Trenches/gaps between zones act as wave shelters. There are 6 wave types, including Super Slow, Lightning, and Beast, which any player triggers by spending 19 Robux. Friends in the lobby give a base boost multiplier, and a Spawn Machine fuses units — (search summary) [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/all-brainrots-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/), [bloxguidesgg](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots), [1v9.gg](https://1v9.gg/blog/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-beginners-guide)
- Events used for money: Cash "Spin of Money", "UFO Wheel", and event lucky blocks — (search summary) [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/roblox-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-rebirth-list-all-requirements-rewards/)

### Inferences
- Rebirth costs are "flat" in Speed (+10 per level), but Speed itself costs 1.17^n cash. Each rebirth therefore costs about 1.17^10 ≈ 4.8x more cash than the last, while the multiplier only adds +0.5x. Late rebirths need base-income and Divine gates on top, the same "named unit" gate used in Steal a Brainrot.
- Zones are distance bands tied to Speed. This is directly relevant to a trench/area design: the trench is the wave shelter, and the Speed threshold decides which area you can reach and get back from.

### Gaps
- No exact cash cost for the first Speed level, so absolute speed prices cannot be computed (only the 1.17x ratio is known).
- No sourced day-1 vs week-1 progress reports were found.
- Wave timing (seconds between waves, wave speeds) was not found.

## Q3. Steal an Egg: egg tiers, income, rebirth, speed

### Takeaway
Steal an Egg gates progress by biome Speed thresholds that grow about 4-40x per biome (Lake 900 up to Angels & Demons 20B). Pets go from $1/s (Chicken) up to about $6.9B/s (Aetheron, Divine). Egg nests reset every in-game night (~4.5 min), with a recurring 5-minute rare-egg race. Whether a rebirth system exists is disputed between sources.

### Cited Findings
- Biome Speed requirements: Forest (start), Lake 900, Desert 10,000, Jungle 40,000 (one list says 50,000), Snow 170,000, Volcano 700,000, Abyss Ocean 2.5M, Prehistoric 18M (some say 17M), Cosmic 700M, Cherry Blossom 2.5B (one says 3.5B), Titan Temple 7B, Angels & Demons 20B. The biggest jump is Prehistoric to Cosmic (38.8x). You can enter any biome, but the guardian chases you once you take an egg, so Speed decides whether you escape. Big eggs slow you by 20% or more, and guides advise being about 20% above the minimum — (search summary) [timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/steal-an-egg-how-to-get-speed-fast), [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-an-egg/steal-an-egg-biomes-and-speed-requirements/)
- Speed sources: treadmill training (base), Trails (multiply training gains), the cash Speed Shop (up to +1B per purchase), Pet Index completion rewards, a free +10,000 Speed at spawn, and a treadmill unlock for the first $1,000 — (search summary) [timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/steal-an-egg-how-to-get-speed-fast)
- Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Cosmic, Secret, Eternal, Divine. 193 pets earning $1/s to $9B/s (Eldorado index). An earlier games.gg article (8 Sep 2026) listed 78 pets — (search summary) [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-an-egg/steal-an-egg-all-pets-index/); (fetched) [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/steal-an-egg-all-eggs-pets-and-income-rates/)
- Income examples: Chicken (Forest, Common) $1/s. Brr Brr Patapim (Forest best) $1.8K/s. Frog (Lake) $3/s. Leviathan $220K/s. Lava Iguana (Volcano, Legendary) $11K/s. Spider (Jungle, Mythic) $22K/s. Ankylosaurus (Prehistoric, Mythic) $120K/s. King Mammoth (Snow, Cosmic) $400K/s. Bronto (Prehistoric, Cosmic) $1.5M/s. Cerberus (Volcano, Secret) $8M/s — (fetched) [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/steal-an-egg-all-eggs-pets-and-income-rates/)
- Endgame: ArchAngel and World Burner each give $5B/s, +600M Speed, and cost about $500B to unlock. Aetheron (Divine, about $6.9B/s) is obtained only by fusing those two — (search summary) [timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/steal-an-egg-divine-pets)
- Nest/egg reset every in-game night, about 4.5 min. The rare-egg race is a recurring 5-minute window — (search summary) [games.gg](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/steal-an-egg-all-eggs-pets-and-income-rates/); (fetched) [stealanegg-wiki.wiki](https://www.stealanegg-wiki.wiki/)
- Rebirth is disputed. bo3.gg says there is no rebirth system. A fan wiki claims a 12 Aug 2026 update added rebirth tiers 5-6 at about +5% passive income each. Another site says these details trace back to auto-generated fan wikis — (search summary) [bo3.gg](https://bo3.gg/games/articles/steal-an-egg-progression-guide), [stealanegg-tools](https://stealanegg-tools.com/rebirth-guide)

### Inferences
- In Steal an Egg, Speed is the main progression currency and cash mostly buys Speed. This is closer to Escape Tsunami than to Steal a Brainrot.
- The roughly 4.5-minute nest refresh plus the 5-minute rare race create a short return loop inside each session.

### Gaps
- No confirmed rebirth costs or rewards, and no treadmill or Speed Shop price list.
- Developer name was not found.

## Q4. Retention levers: events, admin abuse, rotating stock, mutations/traits, offline income

### Takeaway
Steal a Brainrot ships an update almost every Saturday, usually with new brainrots and a limited-time event. Weekly admin-abuse sessions boost mutation and spawn odds. Mutations multiply income from 1.25x to 13x, and stackable traits sit on top. Offline income is capped at 5 hours at a reduced rate, which pulls players back at least twice a day.

### Cited Findings
- Major update almost every Saturday. Examples: Update 13 (24 Aug 2025) added Admin Abuse plus the Admin Lucky Block and Rebirth 14. Update 35 "The Return" (24 Jan 2026) added Rebirth 18. Update 51 (16 May 2026, 1-year anniversary) added a 1YR trait, 30x luck, and an OG Spyder Elephant limited to 25 copies and non-tradeable. Update 53 (13 Jun 2026) made trading permanent and added the Phantom mutation (x12). Update 54 added official in-game "exist counts" and a Trade Plaza. Update 59 (25 Jul 2026) added the Crystal mutation (x13) and Bull trait (6x). Update 61 (8 Aug 2026) added an RNG Machine with upgradable luck and Bee traits (x5 to x6). Update 62 (15 Aug 2026) added R19, the 28th slot, Grief Shield, and removed Lucky Block timers. Update 68 (26 Sep 2026) added 11 Secrets and a limited event — (fetched) [Eldorado update log](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-a-brainrot-update-log/); (search summary) [Fandom Update 35](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Log/Update_35)
- Mutation multipliers: Gold 1.25x (about 10%), Diamond 1.5x (5% or 20.4%, sources conflict), Rainbow 10x (about 1%), then event mutations Bloodrot 2x, Candy 4x, Lava 6x, Galaxy 7x, Yin Yang 7.5x, Radioactive 8.5x, Cursed 9x, Divine 10x, Cyber 11x, Phantom 12x, Crystal 13x. One mutation per brainrot, but traits stack. Mutations raise income without raising belt cost. Wiki formula: income = Rate x Multiplier x Mutation + Trait1 + Trait2... The scheduled "Taco Tuesday" and "Saturday Admin Abuse" sessions raise mutation odds — (search summary) [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-a-brainrot/mutations), [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-mutations-effects-steal-brainrot)
- Offline cash builds automatically after you leave and is capped at 5 hours. The rate is about 3% of normal per the Offline Cash page, or about 50% per the Cash page (the wiki contradicts itself) — (search summary) [Fandom Offline Cash](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Offline_Cash), [Fandom Cash](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Cash)
- Escape Tsunami: a paid "Beast" wave any player can trigger for 19 Robux, cash and UFO wheels, event lucky blocks, and a friend-in-lobby multiplier — (search summary) [bloxguidesgg](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots), [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/roblox-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-rebirth-list-all-requirements-rewards/)

### Inferences
- Content cadence (weekly drops of new top-tier units) keeps raising the ceiling, so players never "finish". New rebirths are added as the top players catch up (R14 in Aug 2025, R18 in Jan 2026, R19 in Aug 2026).
- Scheduled admin-abuse times create appointment play on fixed weekdays.

### Gaps
- Conveyor/shop restock timer values and exact admin-abuse luck multipliers were not found.

## Q5. Player complaints and dev responses

### Takeaway
I found no Reddit or DevForum threads in this pass. Guide sites report that complaints focus on rare-unit walls (R13, R16, R17) and the R19 cash wall. The dev's visible response was halving early and mid rebirth cash costs.

### Cited Findings
- Old vs current cash costs: R1 $1M to $500K, R2 $3M to $1.5M, R9 $25B to $12.5B, R10 $250B to $125B, R11 $1T to $800B, R12 $7T to $3.5T, R14 $100T to $40T, R15 $500T to $100T (-80%). R3 and R4 went up instead ($7.5M to $12.5M, $25M to $35M). Some unit requirements were also moved (R8 used to need Cocofanto Elefanto before the Rebirth 14 update) — (fetched) [U7BUY](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide/); (search summary) [Fandom Rebirth](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth)
- The R19 price was first floated at $45Qa and launched at $30Qa — (search summary) [U7BUY R19](https://www.u7buy.com/blog/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-19/)
- Update 62 removed timers from all Lucky Blocks (a friction cut) — (fetched) [Eldorado update log](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-a-brainrot-update-log/)

### Inferences
- The devs cut cash costs at the early rebirths (R1-R2, so new players reach a rebirth fast) and at the mid stretch (R9-R15). They kept the named-unit gates, which preserves the multi-day pacing.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit, DevForum, or YouTube comment evidence was collected, and no exact dates for the price cuts.
- No complaint or patch data for Escape Tsunami or Steal an Egg.
