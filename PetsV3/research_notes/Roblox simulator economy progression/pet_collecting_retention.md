# Long-term progression and retention in top Roblox pet/collecting simulators

Research date: 2026-10-05. Caveat: the main Pet Simulator Fandom wiki (pet-simulator.fandom.com) returned HTTP 402 to the fetch tool, and progameguides returned 403, so some PS99 numbers come from search snippets and secondary guides. Fan wikis disagree with each other in places; conflicts are flagged.

## Pet Simulator 99: zone cost growth, rebirths, ranks, time to the last zone, update content

### Takeaway
PS99 uses an exponential zone-cost curve. It roughly multiplies cost by about 2.3x per area across World 1. To keep the numbers readable, it moves to a new currency name about every 10 to 25 areas (Coins, then Gold Bars at 1M Coins, then Platinum Coins, Platinum Bars, Emerald, Sapphire). Rebirth statues sit at fixed zone milestones (25/50/75/99...). Each rebirth gives a permanent +75% pet strength plus feature unlocks. The game now has 279 areas across 4 worlds. A separate star-quest Rank system unlocks pet, egg and enchant slots. Weekly updates add limited Huge/Titanic pets, paid Exclusive Eggs and leaderboard events.

- 279 total areas: World 1 (Home/Spawn) areas 1-99; World 2 (Tech) areas 100-199, needs 4 rebirths; World 3 (Void hub) areas 200-239; World 4 (Fantasy) areas 240-279, reached by cannon from Void. Last area is 279 (Hollow Veil Castle) — [HoldToReset areas list, Jan 2026](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-areas-list-and-whats-in-them/)
- "Rebirth Four and the rocketship at Rainbow Road (area 99) lead to Tech World"; "Rebirth Eight and the Black Hole at Void Spiral lead to Void World" — [Bloxodes PS99 areas](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/areas)
- Later worlds (Prison, Millionaire, Kawaii, Elemental, Olympus, Doodle) add task gates: you must complete specific tasks at each gate before you can buy the next area — [HoldToReset](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-areas-list-and-whats-in-them/)

**Update/event content and monetization pattern**
- Patch notes reach at least Update 87 ("Plant & Combine") and Update 92 ("Coin Arcade") on biggames.io. Typical contents: limited Huge and Titanic pets (one update added 2 Titanics for an event), a new paid Exclusive Egg each update (e.g. Kaiju egg with a Gargantuan/Titanic), paid bundles (Dawn Pack: 6 Exclusive Eggs + 40 gifts + Tier 2 boosters), and leaderboard rewards (Top 100 Rainbow Huge / Top 500 Gold Huge / Top 5,000 regular Huge). A rotating merchant buys old Huges for diamonds (a sink) — [BIG Games patch notes, e.g. Update 60/75/76/87](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-87)
- PS99 released December 2023 with 2.5B+ visits. A fan wiki lists about 50k-250k active players (Aug 2026) — [BIG Games Fandom](https://biggames.fandom.com/wiki/Pet_Simulator_99)
- Revenue estimates (unofficial): BIG Games over $100M lifetime. PSX peaked around 4.2M-17M Robux/day and reportedly over $10M from timed exclusive pet bundles alone. Attributed to time-limited exclusives and FOMO — [RoWatcher (secondary analysis)](https://rowatcher.com/news/inside-big-games-how-pet-simulator-became-a-100m-roblox-empire)

### Inferences
- From the World 1 table (assuming 1 Platinum Bar = 1M Platinum Coins, which follows the documented bar/coin pattern), cost runs from about 9e2 coins (area 2) to about 2e19 coins (area 46). That is about 16 orders of magnitude over 44 areas, or about x2.3 per area on average. Early steps are steeper (900 to 2.5k to 30k). Within a currency band it is often about x2-x2.5 per area (100 to 250 Platinum Coins; 100 to 200 to 500 Platinum Bars).
- Design pattern worth copying: rename the currency (or start a fresh currency per world) so displayed numbers stay small. Put rebirths at round-number zones (every 25). Make each rebirth a large flat multiplier (+75%) plus a feature unlock (teleport, auto-hatch, teams, enchant loadout) rather than just a stat. Add task gates in later worlds to slow pacing without inflating numbers further.
- Rank and quest stars form a second axis of progression that drives slot unlocks independently of zone progress, so players always have a short-term goal.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the full, current zone cost table (main Fandom page blocked with 402). Costs for areas 60-199 and the exact currency conversions above Platinum are unverified.
- No reliable source found for "hours to reach the last zone." Community estimates were not found in citable form.
- Rank star requirements per rank beyond Rank 1/10 were not visible in the DB fetch.
- No interview with Preston/BIG Games on retention metrics found. Revenue figures are third-party estimates only.

## Bee Swarm Simulator: bee-count gates, quest chains, long grind items, player lifetime

### Takeaway
Bee Swarm gates the map purely by bee count (5/10/15/20/25/30/35 bee gates) and paces the late game through escalating NPC "bear" quest chains, a 50-bee hive cap, and gifted/mutated hive perfection goals that take players months. Seasonal events such as Beesmas add month-long quest lists.

### Cited Findings
- Gates check only the number of bees: Basic 5 (Spider/Bamboo/Strawberry fields, Panda Bear), Brave 10 (Pineapple, Stump; Science/Dapper/Pro Bear area), Honey 15 (Cactus, Pumpkin, Pine Tree, Rose; Robo and Polar Bear), Ant Gate 20, Lion/Mountain Top 25, Bear Gate 30 (Onett), Windy 35 (Coconut, Pepper, Spirit Bear, Wind Shrine, Petal Shop). Entering without enough bees teleports you back to spawn, except at the Basic gate. Windy and Bear gates need extra purchased hive slots — [BSS Fandom Honey/Windy/Basic gate pages](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Windy_Bee_Gate); [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-bee-swarm-simulator-all-npc-locations/)
- Switchblade Gaming claims gates need specific bee types; the wiki and TheGamer contradict this (bee count only) — [Switchblade](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/roblox/bee-swarm-simulator-guide/)
- Hive maximum is 50 bees. Community pacing: 35 bees at about hive level 8-9, 40 at about lvl 10, 45 at lvl 11-12, 48 at lvl 14, 50 at lvl 15+ — [BSS Fandom progression guide thread](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000682164)
- Endgame goals players track: 100% gifted hive, 100% mutated hive, 50 bees, Gummy Boots, Supreme Saturator — [Endgame Checklist thread](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000797123)
- Endgame quest givers: Spirit Bear (huge pollen, Wind Shrine donations; rewards 3 Spirit Petals), Onett's Star Journey 1-5 (e.g. SJ5: 1 billion each of white/red/blue pollen + 5 King Beetles + 5 Tunnel Bears, reward 100M honey, 10,000 treats, 50 tickets, 1 Star Treat), Dapper Bear as the last quest giver. Gummy Bee unlocks Spirit Bear's chain — [TechWiser Onett quests](https://techwiser.com/bee-swarm-simulator-onett-quests-guide/); [BSS NPC quest DB](https://beeswarmsimulatorcalculator.net/database/npc-quests)
- Grind-length anecdote: one player estimated a single endgame Science Bear quest at 21+ hours over 25 days (25 King Beetle kills, gated by respawn timers) — [BSS Fandom thread](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000678193/r/4400000000002540662)
- Beesmas is a recurring multi-month event with its own long quest list — [Sportskeeda Beesmas guide](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/bee-swarm-simulator-beesmas-event-guide)
- Creator Onett (2018 interview) on design: building a cohesive tone with art, music and bear dialogue; satisfaction from seeing testers "get hooked" — [Roblox Fandom: Onett/Bee Swarm Simulator](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Onett/Bee_Swarm_Simulator)

### Inferences
- Bee-count gating is a clean model for a pet game: gate areas by collection size or pet slots rather than currency, so collecting is the progression. Real-time cooldowns (mob respawns, King Beetle timers) stretch quests across days without needing huge numbers.
- Quest chains from named NPCs, with escalating "collect X billion" targets, are the main late-game time sink.

### Gaps
- No citable data on how many months a typical player stays, or median time to 50 bees / full gifted hive.
- Exact honey cost per hive slot/egg was not collected.

## Grow a Garden: prices, rarity, restocks, mutations, events, offline growth, CCU, weekly updates

### Takeaway
Grow a Garden pairs a shared global 5-minute seed-shop restock with rarity-weighted appearance odds (100% for commons down to about 0.1% for top seeds), offline crop growth, and multiplicative weather/event mutations. Weekly Saturday updates with themed events create appointment play. Each major update set a new Roblox CCU record, peaking at about 22.3M concurrent.

### Cited Findings
- Seed shop restocks every 5 minutes, and the stock is shared by everyone. Instant restock costs 79 Robux (GAG1) and 39 Robux (GAG2). A Common is always in stock. Legendary and higher appear rarely — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/grow-a-garden-seeds-packs/1100-6536143/); [grow-a-garden-2.wiki](https://grow-a-garden-2.wiki/seed-shop/)
- GAG1 seed prices (Sheckles): Carrot 10, Strawberry 50, Blueberry 400, Tomato 800, Corn 1,300, Watermelon 2,500, Pumpkin 3,000, Apple 3.25k, Coconut 6k, Dragon Fruit 50k, Mango 100k, Grape 850k, Pepper 1M, Cacao 2.5M, Beanstalk 10M, Ember Lily 15M, Burning Bud 50M (40M in other sources), Elder Strawberry 70M, Romanesco 88M, Crimson Thorn 10B, Zebrazinkle 21B. Shop odds range from 100% (Common) to about 0.08% (rarest Transcendent), e.g. Burning Bud 0.29%, Crimson Thorn 0.13% — [Beebom, Apr 2026](https://beebom.com/grow-a-garden-seed-shop-guide/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/grow-a-garden-seeds/)
- GAG2 price/appear-rate curve: Carrot 1 (100%), Strawberry 10, Blueberry 25, Tomato 200 (90%), Apple 400 (52%), Corn 2,500 (25%), Cactus 5,000 (16.6%), Mushroom 15,000 (9%), Dragon Fruit 120k (4%), Cherry 1.2M (2.2%), Sunflower 5M (1.7%), Venus Fly Trap 7M (1.43%), Poison Apple 25M (0.5%), Moon Bloom 65M (0.35%), Dragon's Breath 90M (0.275%). Some rates are only "Reported" — [Nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/all-seeds-prices-and-stock-chances-in-grow-a-garden-2); [mygagcalculator stock tracker](https://mygagcalculator.com/grow-a-garden-2-stock-tracker/)
- Crops keep growing offline, which is why sessions are short (about 10.43 min average). Weather/event mutations and pet-applied mutations (Bee to Pollinated, Polar Bear to Chilled) do NOT apply offline, so players come back for weather. The Fairy update's Glimmering can apply offline — [Droid Gamers](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/can-crops-get-mutations-when-youre-offline-in-grow-a-garden/)
- Mutation multipliers stack. A fan database lists about 158 mutations up to Abyssal 240x (counts vary 149-166 across its pages) — [GAGdata mutations](https://www.gagdata.com/mutations)
- Cadence: weekly Saturday patch at about 2 PM UTC (new crops, pets, shop rotation). Major named events every 3-6 weeks, always on a Saturday — [mygagcalculator weekly update](https://mygagcalculator.com/grow-a-garden-weekly-update/); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grow-a-garden/wiki)
- CCU history: Blood Moon update (May 18, 2025) about 5M, nearly double Blox Fruits' record. Bizzy Bees (May 31) 11.7M+. Update 1.10.0 (June 14) 16M+, beating Fortnite's about 14M. Summer update (June 21) 21.3M. Admin-war event with Steal a Brainrot (Aug 23, 2025) 22,346,725. After the June 14 spike it fell back to a daily norm of about 3M — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [RTC on X](https://x.com/Roblox_RTC/status/1959281995453706447); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-game-grow-a-garden-beats-steam-with-record-concurrent-user-high-of-89m/)
- Many concurrent players were AFK with the game open in the background (offline/idle growth encourages leaving the client running) — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/grow-a-garden-player-count-server-limit-and-record-highs/)

### Inferences
- Why the CCU was so high: (1) a global synchronized restock and weather creates the same "everyone log in now" moment across all servers. (2) A Saturday release time turned updates into weekly live events. (3) Offline growth lowers the cost of a session while weather-only mutations reward being present. (4) AFK-friendly play inflates CCU.
- Price curve: roughly x2-x10 per tier among cheap seeds, with appearance odds falling as price rises. Scarcity plus stock that sells out in seconds drives Robux restocks.

### Gaps
- No official D1/D7 figures for Grow a Garden. No developer (Jandel) interview with retention metrics found within budget.
- Gear prices were not collected.

## Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity: worlds, rebirth/prestige, egg odds, secret pets

### Takeaway
BGSI has 3 permanent worlds plus 10+ rotating LIMITED worlds. Its long tail comes from astronomically rare Secret pets (1 in 1M to 1 in 100B base), luck stacking, 10-minute rifts, a 3-4 week Bubble Pass with hourly and daily challenges, Mastery/Milestones, and Friday weekly updates. It has no classic account-wide rebirth. Rebirth-style loops live inside event worlds.

### Cited Findings
- 3 permanent worlds (Overworld, Minigame Paradise, Seven Seas) and 10 past LIMITED worlds. Overworld zones: Surface, Floating Island, Outer Space, Twilight, The Void, Zen — [BGSI Fandom Worlds](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Worlds)
- Minigame Paradise needs Zen unlocked and costs 1B coins (cut from 10B in Update 5, May 25, 2025). It has 5 zones at altitude thresholds 2,860 / 7,625 / 13,355 / 20,030 m and uses Tickets to enter minigames on cooldowns — [BGSI Fandom Minigame Paradise](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Minigame_Paradise)
- Secret pet base odds range from 1 in 1,000,000 to 1 in 100,000,000,000, improved by Luck and Secret Luck. Examples: King Doggy 1/100M, The Overlord 1/50M, Godly Gem 1/250M, Dementor 1/1B. The limited "Rebirth" pet was 1 in 10B (XL variant 1 in 5T), available March 20 - May 22, 2026, now trade-only — [BGSI Fandom Pets](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Pets); [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-pet-guide/)
- Rebirth Machine: combine 5 Secret pets of the same variant into a unique Secret. Rotating machines with set odds (e.g. 50% / 30% / 18.9% / 1%), each running about 3-7 weeks — [BGSI Fandom Rebirth Machine](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth_Machine)
- Rifts: temporary islands lasting 10 min (30 if summoned from Bubble Shrine). Egg/chest/gift rifts and rare ones (Gum Rift 1/200 spawn) — [BGSI Fandom Rifts](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Rifts)
- Bubble Pass: 3-4 weeks, hourly challenges (reset every hour) plus daily challenges (reset 00:00 UTC), both skippable for Robux. An infinite track grants stacking perks up to +1500% Season Luck, +250% Luck, +20% hatch speed — [BGSI Fandom Bubble Pass](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Bubble_Pass)
- Milestones and Mastery are long-term trackers (Mastery unlocks e.g. Rift Summon) — [BGSI Fandom Milestones](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Milestones)
- Prestige conflict: one guide claims an "Infinity Shards" altitude prestige. A later guide (Carnival update, July 2026) says there is no account-wide reset beyond Mastery, and that rebirth exists only inside the Carnival event world (Rebirth Tokens) — [earnaldo guide](https://earnaldo.com/blog/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity); [earnaldo July 2026](https://earnaldo.com/blog/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-update-july-2026)
- Roblox page: "Updates every Friday at 2PM EST" — [Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/85896571713843/Bubble-Gum-Simulator-INFINITY)

### Inferences
- BGSI swaps rebirth resets for luck stacking toward ultra-rare chase pets, plus short-lived limited content (limited worlds, limited secrets, rotating combine machines). Hourly pass challenges give a reason to come back several times a day.

### Gaps
- Overworld zone unlock costs and the World 3 (Seven Seas) cost were not collected. No CCU/retention numbers gathered.

## Common retention mechanics and reported D1/D7/D30 benchmarks

### Takeaway
The best benchmark data (GameAnalytics 2025) puts median Roblox D1 at about 4-11% and D7 at about 0.4-1.6%, depending on session length. Hits (19+ min sessions) reach about 13-20% D1 and about 3% D7. Several smaller blogs report 2-4x higher numbers, which look unreliable. The retention toolkit the top games share: daily streaks with streak protection, offline/AFK progress that requires check-ins, weekly fixed-day updates, limited/FOMO items, battle passes with hourly and daily quests, leaderboards with exclusive prizes, collection indexes, and trading.

### Cited Findings
**Benchmarks**
- GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox report (by avg session length, 50th / 75th percentile): 0-3 min D1 4.31% / 7.44%, D7 0.41% / 1.02%. 7-12 min D1 7.91% / 9.82%, D7 0.90% / 1.46%. 13-18 min D1 10.24% / 12.16%, D7 1.20% / 1.80%. 19-24 min D1 11.46% / 13.55%, D7 1.61% / 2.79%. Games with 19+ min sessions have double-digit D1 and about 2 sessions/day — [GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox Benchmark](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2025-roblox-report)
- InvestGame summary: top performers keep 15-20% D1. In weaker brackets even the best keep under 5% at D7 and under 1.5% at D30 — [InvestGame](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/the-2025-roblox-benchmark-report/)
- Developer anecdote: a 2026 breakout game had about 18% D1 and grew from about 7k to 126k DAU in 8 days — [EvanZir on X](https://x.com/EvanZir/article/2074281929575174603)
- Higher, less rigorous figures: BLOXG claims Simulator 32% D1 / 14% D7 (850+ promoted games). RoLearn says 50%+ D1 is top-5%. Both are 2-4x GameAnalytics' upper percentiles and likely use different definitions — [BLOXG](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-retention-benchmarks); [RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/first-week-retention-optimization/)
- Roblox's own guidance: D1 and average session time are the first metrics to focus on, and D7/D30 show long-term progression — [Roblox Creator Docs Analytics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics)

**Adopt Me retention systems (official)**
- Daily login streak earns Stars for the Star Rewards shop. The streak is shown on a sign outside the player's house (social display). Pets+ subscribers never lose their streak. Others can buy a 24h Streak Saver for 5 Stars (stackable) — [Uplift Games Daily Streak FAQ](https://upliftgames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39533852927892-Daily-Streak-FAQ)
- Long-term pet aging: newborn to full grown through needs/tasks. 4 full-grown pets make a Neon, and 4 full-grown Neons make a Mega Neon (16 pets' worth of aging). Mega Neons launched Apr 17, 2020 with a 2x aging weekend — [Adopt Me Mega Neons notes](https://www.playadopt.me/news/mega-neons-update-notes-adopt-me-on-roblox)
- Pet Pen (Sept 2025): ages 4 pets while AFK/offline at 8 needs/hr online (800 XP) vs 2 needs/hr offline (200 XP). Pets can only gain one level in the pen before the player must visit, which forces check-ins — [Adopt Me Pet Pen notes](https://www.playadopt.me/news/pet-pen-and-pet-releaser-features-notes)
- Updates release on Fridays. Player counts usually triple on major updates. 40.8B+ visits as of Nov 2025 — [Wikipedia Adopt Me!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!)

### Inferences
- Shared retention stack across these titles: (1) a fixed weekly update day and time; (2) limited-time pets/eggs that become trade-only, keeping value for collectors and traders; (3) a daily streak with paid or earned protection; (4) offline progress capped so players must check in (Pet Pen one-level cap, GAG weather-only mutations); (5) a pass or quest track with hourly and daily resets; (6) leaderboard events with tiered exclusive variants; (7) indexes/milestones/mastery as completion goals; (8) zone or rebirth gates that unlock features, not just stats.
- For a new simulator, a realistic target is D1 above 10% and D7 above 1.5-3% (GameAnalytics upper percentiles), not the 30%+ blog figures.
