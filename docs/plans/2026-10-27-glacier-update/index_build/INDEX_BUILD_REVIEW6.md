## MUST

**M1. The card name touches the pet's head on the S22, iPhone 13 and iPhone 17 Pro.**
- Captures 31 (S22, PERMA FROST), 32 (iPhone 13, PERMAFROST TITAN) and 33 (iPhone 17 Pro, PERMAFROST TITAN). Zoomed at 8x, the name's bottom outline sits about 0-1 px above the top of the purple pet, and on the S22 the pet's top edge is at the name line.
- Cause: the round 6 side fix moved the phone card name 0.005 lower. It clears the SECRET badge, but now it lands on the portrait. The same touch kind as the review 2 CLAIM! MUST.
- Fix (same layout, phones only): move the portrait down about 2 px, or scale it to about 0.96 inside its card, so the name's bottom outline has at least 2 px of clear space. Keep the chance pill at least 2 px below the portrait. The lead has the numbers.
- Do not move the name back up. That would reopen the SECRET and MYTHIC gaps.
- Verify with the same measurement on all three phones.
- The 16 Pro Max (about 2-3 px clear) does not need this change.
