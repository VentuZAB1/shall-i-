## Verdict

**PASS on the score bar, with owner sign-off still needed.** Every device meets the owner's bar: four at 8.5 or above, and the S22 at 8.4 against its 8.3 floor. There are no MUSTs left for 8.5. Two phone-only visible changes still need the owner's yes before Save and Publish (see below).

## What works

- **Review 6 MUST fixed.** The card name clears the pet's head on every phone. In zooms of captures 36, 39, 40, 37 and 38, each name sits a few pixels above its pet. The S22 all-42 Shadow row (capture 38) also clears, including PHARAOH, BLACK EASTERN and ABYSS WEAVER.
- **Names.** FROSTCROWN and PERMAFROST are shortened on phones and read clearly. The S22 all-42 Shadow tab shows shortened names with the full "PHARAOH ASPARUH" in the inspect panel, as designed. PC keeps full names.
- **Placement.** On all four phones the window sits below the top bar, clears the screen edges and the money and speed pills, and keeps a bottom margin. The 16 Pro Max window is narrowed to fit beside the pills, as built.
- **Footer, chips and badges.** CLAIM!, CLAIM ALL, the chips, the count badge, SECRET and MYTHIC are clean on all four phones.
- **Labels.** The coordinator measures no label under 9 px on the iPhone 13, 17 Pro and 16 Pro Max, and the smallest on the S22 at 8 px. I have not re-measured these. They are consistent with what the captures show.

## MUST

None for the 8.5 bar, and none for the S22's 8.3 floor.

## Owner sign-off (visible changes, not score blockers)

1. **Short phone names.** FROSTCROWN, PERMAFROST, and other long names on phones. Per the owner's rule, a visible change needs a yes. Captures 37, 38, 39 and 36 show it. The inspect panel always shows the full name.
2. **Phone portrait is smaller than PC.** The portrait height on phones is 0.63 of the card, against 0.68 on PC. Visibly, the phone pets look about 7 % smaller than PC. The owner's one-layout rule says the phone is the PC layout scaled. This is a phone-only proportion inside the same boxes, not a rearranged layout, but it is not an exact scale of PC. The owner should accept it with item 1, or say which the owner prefers.

## NICE

1. **S22 labels at 8 px.** Ten labels sit at 8 px: the four reward chips, the "!" alerts, SECRET, CLAIM ALL, the subtitle, BONUS and the count. They read clearly at 2x. Lifting them to 9 px needs the container changes the owner has been asked about before. This is the main reason the S22 stays at 8.4 rather than higher.
2. **Chance pill overlaps the bottom of the pet.** On every device the pill covers the lower edge of the portrait, most visibly the Pharaoh's base and the Black Eastern's tail on the S22 (capture 38). This is v8's design, and the overlap on phones is about 0.015 of the card larger than PC because the portrait bottom moved from 0.90 to 0.915. Small, and not a blocker.
3. **Glacier portraits look washed out (PC and phones).** Unchanged from reviews 2 to 6. The Ice Mammoth on the pale card is the clearest case. This is the main reason PC stays at 8.8. The builder's fix is a black outline or a 256 px downscale.
4. **Jump button under the open window (phones).** Unchanged. On the S22 the window covers about the left third of the jump button, and the arrow stays visible.
5. **PC capture size.** Capture 35 is 1372 x 686, not the 1519 x 753 viewport the coordinator gave. It is enough to judge the window and its text, but the placement of the window on PC is judged from this smaller image.
6. **Unrelated HUD on the S22 capture.** Capture 38 shows a "MEDIUM" pill at the top right, outside the Index. Not part of this build, so noted only so it is not mistaken for an Index defect.
